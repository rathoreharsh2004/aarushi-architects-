01 — Studio

Designing the way you live.

Aarushi Maharshi Architects is an architectural design practice working between Jaipur and Ahmedabad. Our approach begins with understanding people, place, proportion and material.

Rather than following a fixed visual language, every project develops its own identity — shaped by climate, context, function and the personality of the people who inhabit it.
